    HOW TO PLAY

    Each round the gun is loaded with live and blank shells. You're told how many of each, then they go in — in an order nobody knows.

    On your turn, use as many items as you like, then point the gun at yourself or another player.

    • Live shell: whoever it's pointed at loses 1 HP.
    • Blank at yourself: you go again.
    • Blank at someone else: your turn ends.

    When the gun is empty a new round starts. Last player standing wins.

    Items — Progressive: round 1 has none, round 2 gives 2 each, round 3 gives 3… Draft: everyone picks from one shared tray. Shop: survive rounds to earn chips, then buy. You hold up to 8 items; anything that doesn't fit is lost.
